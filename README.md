Eco Friendly Products- Consumer Awareness & Behaviour Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze consumer awareness, perceptions, and purchasing behavior toward eco-friendly products. The dashboard is based on survey data and provides insights into demographic trends, awareness levels, purchase patterns, and factors influencing consumer decisions.

## Objectives

* Analyze consumer awareness of eco-friendly products.
* Understand purchasing behavior and frequency.
* Identify factors influencing purchase decisions.
* Examine the impact of social media and environmental claims on consumer perception.
* Explore willingness to pay a premium for sustainable products.

## Dashboard Pages

1. Overview Dashboard

The overview page presents key demographic and awareness-related insights across 2 dashboard pages containing 12+ visualizations, including:

* Total survey respondents
* Consumer awareness percentage
* Sources of awareness
* Gender distribution
* Educational qualification distribution
* Age distribution
* Advertisement influence on consumer perception
* Key insights summary

2. Consumer Behaviour Analysis

This page focuses on consumer attitudes and purchasing patterns:

* Purchase frequency analysis
* Premium willingness percentage
* Trust in eco-friendly claims
* Social media influence on perception
* Understanding of eco-friendly products
* Key factors influencing purchasing decisions

## Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query, including:

* Handling missing values
* Splitting multiple-response survey answers
* Standardizing categorical responses
* Creating custom sorting tables for survey response categories
* Building relationships between tables for accurate analysis

## Tools Used

* Power BI Desktop
* Power Query
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Microsoft Excel

## Key Insights

• 83.48% of respondents are aware of eco-friendly products, yet only 35.65% are willing to pay a premium for them- suggesting awareness alone doesn't drive premium purchasing

• Quality (71) and Price (61) are the top purchase drivers, suggesting consumers want eco-friendly products to compete on mainstream standards. They were ranked above environmental concern (55)

• Only 33.04% trust eco-friendly claims while 45.22% remain unsure — revealing a significant credibility gap

• 52 respondents purchase eco-friendly products only occasionally, indicating awareness has not converted to consistent buying habit

• Social media was the top awareness source (45 of 115 respondents), followed by educational institutions (35)
